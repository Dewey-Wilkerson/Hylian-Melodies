Read `AGENTS.md` first: it holds the rules, layout, commands and traps for this repository. Then
`docs/PROGRESS.md` (status and next steps), `docs/KNOWN-ISSUES.md`, `docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `docs/DECISIONS.md`.

(ordinary pushes of `main` to `origin` are OK'd); no sudo and no
writes outside the project, `~/.cache/hylian-melodies/`, `~/.local/share/hylian-melodies/`, `~/refs/`; no GPL/AGPL
code without approval; call hactool only through `installer/hactool.py`.
